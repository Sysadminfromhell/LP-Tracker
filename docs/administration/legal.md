# Privacy & Legal Pages

The application includes editable public legal pages:

| Page | Route |
| --- | --- |
| Privacy Policy | `/privacy` |
| Processed Data | `/privacy/data` |
| Your Rights | `/privacy/rights` |
| Imprint | `/imprint` |

Initial templates are **unpublished** by default. Review them in the admin interface under **Privacy and Imprint** before enabling public publication.

Replace deployment placeholders with correct operator and contact details. Review hosting, provider usage, logging, retention periods, international data transfers and the legal basis for your actual installation.

The supplied pages are templates, not a substitute for legal review. Updating your hosting or provider configuration may require updating the published texts.
